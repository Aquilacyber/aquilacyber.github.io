# Compliance Analyst

Last reviewed: 2026-10-03

[All roles](README.md)

## Summary

Checks that an organisation does what its policies, its customers' contracts and the law require, and keeps the proof. A compliance analyst maps controls to frameworks such as ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2 and PCI DSS, collects evidence, prepares for audits, tracks fixes until they close and reviews vendors. In Nigeria the work also covers the Nigeria Data Protection Act and, for banks and payment companies, the CBN's cybersecurity requirements.

Compliance is one of the most accessible ways into security for people from banking, law, audit or operations.

## Hard Skills

- How major frameworks are built and how their controls overlap. See [frameworks and standards](../guides/frameworks-and-standards.md)
- Evidence collection: screenshots, exports, logs and approvals that prove a control works
- Maintaining a risk register and a control library
- Writing and reviewing policies, standards and procedures
- Vendor and third-party risk questionnaires
- Spreadsheets used well, and in larger companies a GRC platform such as ServiceNow GRC, Archer, Vanta or Drata
- Basics of data protection law and breach notification duties

## Soft Skills

- Asking clear questions of busy engineers and following up without friction
- Accuracy. An audit finding often comes from one wrong date or one missing approval
- Writing plainly for people who are not technical
- Holding a line politely when a team wants to skip a control

## Education

Degrees in IT, business, law, accounting or economics all lead here. No single degree is required.

## How to get there

1. Follow the [GRC track](../tracks/grc.md).
2. Build the risk register and the NDPA gap assessment described there.
3. Learn one framework well and be able to map its controls to another.
4. Apply for compliance, risk or IT audit support roles, and look at fintech and consulting firms that serve regulated clients.

## Certifications

ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity and CompTIA Security+ are entry options. ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer or Lead Auditor courses are common in consulting. CISA, CISM and CRISC from ISACA need work experience. See the [certifications guide](../guides/certifications.md).

## Interview Questions

- What is the difference between a policy, a standard and a procedure?
- How would you prepare a team for an ISO 27001 audit in three months?
- An engineer says a control is in place but cannot show evidence. What do you do?
- Which parts of the NDPA apply to a company that stores customer ID numbers?
- How would you rate and report a risk to a non-technical manager?
