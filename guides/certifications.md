# Certifications

A certification is a receipt for knowledge you already have. It can get your CV past a filter and it can force you to finish a syllabus. It cannot replace being able to do the work.

## Before you buy

1. Look at ten job adverts for the role you want. Count which certifications they name.
2. Check the current exam code and price on the vendor's own site. Codes change every few years.
3. Check the price in Naira, not just dollars.
4. Ask whether an employer, a university or a scholarship scheme will pay.
5. Make sure you can already pass the practice exams without memorising answers.

## Entry level

| Certification | Issuer | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) | ISC2 | Beginners from any background | Entry-level, no experience required |
| Security+ | CompTIA | Blue team, general IT security | SY0-701 is the current version. CompTIA lists a new version, SY0-801, for launch on or around 17 November 2026. Check which one to sit |
| Network+ | CompTIA | People weak on networking | Optional. The N10-009 syllabus is useful even if you skip the exam |
| Google Cybersecurity Certificate | Google, on Coursera | Beginners who want a structured course | A course certificate, not a proctored exam |

## Red team

| Certification | Issuer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| eJPT | INE | Practical, entry-level penetration testing |
| PNPT | TCM Security | Practical exam that includes a report and a debrief |
| PenTest+ | CompTIA | Multiple choice plus performance questions, currently PT0-003 |
| OSCP | OffSec | Well known, hard and fully practical. Do it after you have real practice |

CEH from EC-Council appears in many job adverts. Its value for learning is debated among practitioners. If the roles you want ask for it, you may need it. If not, spend the money elsewhere.

## Renewal

Most certifications expire unless you renew them with continuing education credits or a new exam. CompTIA certifications, for example, are valid for three years. Read the renewal rules before you buy.
