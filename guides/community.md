# Community

AquilaCyber is a Nigerian cybersecurity learning community. Learning alone is slow. People who learn in a group usually finish more.

## Join

Start at [aquilacyber.org](https://www.aquilacyber.org/). The site has the current ways to join and the latest announcements.

## What to expect

- Other learners at every stage, from people on phase 1 to working professionals
- Study groups and practical sessions
- Mentorship programmes. AquilaCyber has run Gurls n Security, a one-month cybersecurity mentorship programme. Check the website for current intakes
- A place to share write-ups and get feedback

## How to ask for help

A good question gets an answer. A vague one does not.

1. Say what you are trying to do.
2. Say what you tried, with the exact commands.
3. Paste the error message as text, not a photo, when you can.
4. Say what you expected to happen.
5. Say what you already searched for.

When someone answers, tell them whether it worked. That helps the next person who has the same problem.

## Share your work

Post your write-ups and projects. Ask for one specific piece of feedback, such as "Is my report clear to a non-technical reader?" Specific questions get useful answers.

## Help other people

- Answer questions from learners one phase behind you. Explaining something is the quickest way to find out whether you understand it.
- Fix a broken link or an unclear sentence in this repository. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Run a short session on something you learned.

## Conduct

Be honest and respectful. Do not ask for help to attack a system you do not have permission to test, and do not share stolen data or malware. Read the [ethics and law guide](ethics-and-law.md). People who ignore it are removed from the community.
