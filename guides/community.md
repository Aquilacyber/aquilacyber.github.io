# Community

Last reviewed: 2026-10-03

AquilaCyber is a community-driven cybersecurity learning initiative for people across Africa and beyond, with more than 1,000 members. Learning alone is slow. People who learn in a group usually finish more.

## What AquilaCyber runs

The list below was taken from the [programmes page](https://www.aquilacyber.org/programs) in October 2026. Check that page for what is live now and for anything new.

- **Hack It Together (HIT).** The flagship group mentorship programme, with Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced tracks. Beginners work with mentors on challenges, write-ups and CTF-style tasks. Apply at [aquilacyber.org/hit](https://www.aquilacyber.org/hit).
- **AquilaCyber Defenders.** A network of university clubs. Campus chapters run local events, workshops and CTFs. See [aquilacyber.org/defenders](https://www.aquilacyber.org/defenders) for chapters.
- **Defenders Portal.** AquilaCyber's own capture the flag platform. See the [Defenders Portal guide](defenders-portal.md).
- **Game Night.** Regular community game nights that mix friendly competition with security concepts.
- **Intro to Cyber.** This repository.

## How to ask for help

A good question gets an answer. A vague one does not.

1. Say what you are trying to do.
2. Say what you tried, with the exact commands.
3. Paste the error message as text, not a photo, when you can.
4. Say what you expected to happen.
5. Say what you already searched for.

When someone answers, tell them whether it worked. That helps the next person who has the same problem.

## Share your work

Post your write-ups and projects and ask for one specific piece of feedback, such as "Is my report clear to a non-technical reader?" Specific questions get useful answers. The website mentions public write-ups from the community. Ask in the group how to contribute yours. The [write-up template](../templates/writeup-template.md) is a good start.

## Help other people

- Answer questions from learners one phase behind you. Explaining something is the quickest way to find out whether you understand it.
- Fix a broken link or an unclear sentence in this repository. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Run a short session on something you learned.
- If you are at a university, find out whether your campus has a Defenders chapter.

## Conduct

Read the AquilaCyber [Terms of Conduct](https://www.aquilacyber.org/terms). Do not ask for help to attack a system you do not have permission to test, and do not share stolen data or malware. Read the [ethics and law guide](ethics-and-law.md).
