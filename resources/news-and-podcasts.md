# News and podcasts

Last reviewed: 2026-10-03

Ten minutes a day of security news teaches you the vocabulary and shows you how real incidents unfold. You do not need to read everything. Pick two sources and stay with them.

## News and blogs

- [Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/): investigative reporting on cybercrime.
- [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/): news on breaches, malware and vulnerabilities.
- [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/): daily news with technical detail.
- [SANS Internet Storm Center](https://isc.sans.edu/): daily handler diaries on current threats. Good for blue team learners.
- [CISA alerts](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories): advisories on active vulnerabilities and campaigns.

## Podcasts

- [Darknet Diaries](https://darknetdiaries.com/): true stories of hacks and the people involved. A good place to start.
- [Risky Business](https://risky.biz/): a weekly news discussion for working professionals.
- [Security Now](https://twit.tv/shows/security-now): a long-running weekly show that explains security topics in depth.

## Nigeria

Follow advisories from ngCERT and announcements from the NDPC and the CBN. The [Nigeria guide](../guides/nigeria.md) explains what each body does.
