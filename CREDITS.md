## Role profiles

The [careers](careers/) folder holds 37 role profiles. Twenty-eight were compiled from public job descriptions and rewritten, and the source of the original text is not recorded. Nine profiles, and the interview questions and training resources on every profile, are original.

If you are the author of any of this material and want it credited differently or taken down, open an issue and it will be handled quickly.
